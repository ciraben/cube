magic, but do it *fast*

tl;dr - it's blitz magic (timed!) but ONLY THE ACTIVE PLAYER GETS PRIORITY

* choose a starting player & resolve mulligans before you start.
* as the starting player begins their turn, start the clock!
* only the active player gets priority
  * (you become the active player as your turn begins)
  * conveniently, with priority obsolete, we can fill this void in our vocabulary with clock instead!
  * you "have clock" when your clock is actively counting down. you can also receive clock, pass clock, hold clock - it's lovely! none of this affects priority. it's just fun to say🌸
* the active player passes clock once after moving to the "declare blockers" step, and once after ending the turn.
  * this also means that, unless a player decides to attack, they retain clock throughout their combat step
  * (you skip the "declare blockers" step if no creature attacks or is put onto the battlefield attacking)
  * (ending the turn includes discarding to hand-size)
* the defending player passes clock after declaring blockers and adding any resulting triggered abilities they control to the stack.

* before passing clock, as well as before taking any action while holding clock, a player must first:
  * finish resolving all spells and abilities they control,
  * ensure that they have accurately represented the current game-state on their side of the battlefield (eg. moving cards between zones, updating their life total, updating counters & tokens, clearly tapped and untapped permanents, etc), and
  * have hands off all game pieces other than their hand
* non-active players can and are encouraged to help update game-state
